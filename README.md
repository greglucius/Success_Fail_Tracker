# Accomplishments Tracker

A simple, browser-based tool for recording your professional wins throughout the year. Use it to build your case for promotions, raises, and resume updates.

## Getting Started

Open `index.html` in any modern browser. That's it — no server, no install, no dependencies.

Your data is stored in your browser's localStorage and persists between sessions.

## Features

- **Add accomplishments** with title, description, date, category, impact level, and tags
- **Categories**: Technical, Leadership, Collaboration, Innovation, Delivery, Mentoring, Process Improvement, Customer Impact, Other
- **Impact levels**: High, Medium, Low — color-coded on each card
- **Search & filter** by keyword, category, impact, and year
- **Export** in three formats:
  - **Markdown** — great for docs and notes
  - **Plain text** — for email or simple copy/paste
  - **Resume bullets** — ready to drop into your resume
- **Year Summary** — see totals, impact breakdown, category distribution, and monthly activity

## Tips

- Record accomplishments as they happen — don't wait until review season
- Include metrics when possible (e.g., "reduced build time by 35%")
- Use tags to make searching easier later
- Export before your performance review to have everything organized
